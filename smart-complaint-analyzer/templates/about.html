ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for more responsive communities

Smart Complaint Analyzer is a student-built academic prototype for managing civic complaints with NLP and machine learning. It is not affiliated with any municipal authority.

Our objective

Reduce manual sorting, surface urgent issues for human review, identify potentially similar reports, and give citizens visibility into status changes.

Recommendations are automated estimates and are not official emergency assessments.
